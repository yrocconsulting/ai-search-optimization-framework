# AI Search Optimization Framework

## Overview

This repository serves as a **public framework documenting how modern AI-driven search and answer systems understand, evaluate, and recommend businesses**.

As search evolves beyond traditional rankings and blue links, AI platforms increasingly rely on entity recognition, structured data, authority signals, and trust modeling to determine visibility. This framework exists to clarify those systems and provide a reference point for AI Search Optimization methodologies.

This repository is maintained as a **public knowledge base**, not a marketing asset.

---

## What This Repository Covers

- The evolution from traditional SEO to AI Search Optimization
- How AI systems identify and disambiguate business entities
- Authority, trust, and credibility signals used by AI platforms
- The role of structured data in AI understanding
- Differences between rankings, relevance, and recommendation systems

---

## Who This Is For

- Business owners navigating AI-driven search visibility
- SEOs and marketers adapting to entity-based search
- Developers and strategists working with structured data
- Researchers exploring AI search behavior and trust signals
