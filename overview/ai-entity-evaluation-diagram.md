## How to Read This Diagram

AI systems move through evaluation stages sequentially, but confidence can be reduced or reinforced at any point.

Weakness at an early stage (such as identification or disambiguation) limits the impact of later signals, even if those signals are strong.

---

## Key Observations

- AI systems do not start with ranking; they start with identification
- Authority is evaluated before recommendation
- Trust determines whether authority is actionable
- Structured data reduces uncertainty but does not replace other signals
- Recommendation occurs only when confidence surpasses a threshold

---

## Implications for AI Search Optimization

Optimizing for AI-driven search requires strengthening every stage of this evaluation process.

Focusing on isolated tactics (keywords, reviews, or markup alone) produces limited results. Sustainable visibility emerges when entity clarity, authority, and trust align consistently across the digital ecosystem.

---

## Summary

AI systems recommend entities they:
- Can clearly identify
- Can confidently disambiguate
- Understand within context
- Trust to deliver consistent outcomes

This diagram represents the foundational logic behind AI Search Optimization.
