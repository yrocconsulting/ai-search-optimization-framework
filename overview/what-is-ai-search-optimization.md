# What Is AI Search Optimization?

AI Search Optimization (AISO) is the practice of structuring digital presence so that AI-driven search and answer systems can accurately understand, trust, and recommend a business or entity.

Unlike traditional SEO, which primarily targets keyword rankings and links, AI Search Optimization focuses on:

- Entity recognition and disambiguation
- Authority and trust modeling
- Structured data and semantic clarity
- Consistency across digital ecosystems

AI systems do not "rank" businesses in the traditional sense. They evaluate entities based on confidence, credibility, and contextual relevance.

If an AI system cannot confidently understand who a business is, what it does, and whether it can be trusted, it will not recommend that business — regardless of rankings.

This framework exists to document those mechanisms.
